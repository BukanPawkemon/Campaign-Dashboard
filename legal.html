Dalang lets you create your own homebrew monsters, magic items, and equipment, and lets you write your own campaign content (locations, NPCs, quests, lore, notes).

All homebrew and user-generated content is created by you, the user, and is your own responsibility. This content is not reviewed, moderated, verified, or endorsed by the creator of Dalang. If you choose to share your homebrew or campaign files with others, you are responsible for what you share.

5. Privacy (Dalang Desktop)

Dalang Desktop runs entirely on your own device. All campaign data is stored locally in a file on your computer.

  • No account is required to use the desktop app
  • No campaign data, homebrew content, or personal information is collected, transmitted, or stored on any server
  • The creator of Dalang has no access to your campaign data unless you choose to share it directly (for example, sending an exported file to someone)

App fonts are bundled with the app, so the desktop app does not contact any font CDN at startup.

6. Dalang Web (Privacy & Terms)

Dalang Web is the browser version at app.dalang.page. Unlike the desktop app, it uses an account and stores your campaigns on a server, so it has its own privacy and terms.

What Dalang Web stores

  • Your account email and username, held by the authentication provider (Supabase) so you can sign in.
  • Your campaigns and everything in them, in the project database (Supabase, Singapore region).
  • Technical logs from the hosting providers (Cloudflare and Supabase), including IP addresses, kept to run and secure the service.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (Google Analytics), only if you accept the cookie notice. It records structural events like "a campaign was created", never the content of your campaigns. You can decline and use the app normally.
  • When you register, the fact that you agreed to these terms, with the date.

Do not put real personal data into your campaigns. Dalang is for fictional game content. We do not sell your data, use your campaign content to train anything, or share it with anyone beyond the infrastructure providers above.

Your rights

Export a full copy of any campaign at any time from Campaign Settings. To close your account and delete your campaigns, or for any other request, email support@dalang.page. You must be at least 13 to create an account.
