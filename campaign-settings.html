Campaign Settings

Settings · Campaign Settings

Rename the campaign or its adventure title, set a campaign image, and manage a few campaign-wide things:

  • Background Image: shown behind the Active Campaign hub, and as the fallback for the Player Window's idle screen, whenever the current location doesn't have an image of its own. This is per-campaign, separate from DM Settings' own background image, which shows behind My Campaigns and the Library instead, see Getting Started.
  • Export Campaign: saves the campaign to a file. Choose Adventure only (world content, for sharing with another DM, leaves out your session history and players) or Full campaign (everything, for a backup or for moving to another device). The same file opens in Dalang Desktop or Dalang Web. To bring one back in, use New Campaign on the My Campaigns screen or Import on the Library screen.
  • Build Player Guide: a DM-curated standalone file for your players, HTML or PDF, see Player Guide.
  • Save to Library: keeps a reusable copy of the world content you can spin up new campaigns from later.
  • Delete Campaign: in the danger zone at the bottom, requires typing a confirmation phrase. There's no undo, so make sure you've exported anything worth keeping first.
Dalang Web runs all of this from a browser, free during the beta, with a live read-only link for your players.
